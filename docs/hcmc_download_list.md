# Ho Chi Minh City land price table: manual download list

The annexes of Resolution 87/2025/NQ-HĐND (land prices by street segment, in force 1 Jan 2026)
are published across the Official Gazette issues below. The gazette blocks automated
downloads, so they are downloaded by hand in a browser.

For each issue: open the link, find **Nghị quyết 87/2025/NQ-HĐND** in the list of documents,
click **Tải về** (download), and save the PDF into `data/raw/hcmc/` without renaming it.
Issues that do not list Resolution 87 can be skipped.

All 12 parts were downloaded on 2026-11-08 (issues 62+63 to 84+85 of 01-03-2026); the table is complete.
