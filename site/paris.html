The valuation gap compares the model's predicted market price with rents capitalised at an assumed user cost. A positive value means prices are above what rents justify under the baseline assumptions; see the sensitivity band below. It is a model-dependent hypothesis, not investment advice.

Relative mispricing is the median residual of the price model: positive = dearer than comparable dwellings elsewhere in the city, negative = cheaper. It says nothing about a bubble.

Two cross-checks of sale prices

Implausible
Check 1, rent side, 2025: is the rent growth the price requires plausible?
Sale prices require rents to grow by 4.0% a year (3.0% to 6.0% for a risk premium of 1% to 4%). Over the last 20 years: rents 1.4% a year and incomes 2.4% a year.
Beyond capacity
Check 2, income side, 2025: can a local household finance the price?
The median sale price is 1.56 times what the reference household can finance (1.39 to 1.73 with the rate one point lower or higher; rate used 3.0%).

Check 1 solves the rent-capitalisation formula for the growth of rents instead of the value; incomes are the ceiling, since rents cannot outgrow them for ever. Check 2 ignores rents: it applies the lending rules (35% of income, 25 years, 20% down payment (HCSF rule; the down payment is a convention), at the average rate on new French home loans) to a couple at the median disposable income of the neighbourhood (INSEE Filosofi 2021, held at its 2021 level), 60 m². Read together for 2025: fails both checks. Both checks move with interest rates, and check 2 describes a median local household, not the people who actually buy. They are reported next to the verdict and are not part of its rule. Model-dependent hypotheses, not investment advice.

Prices and ratios through past cycles

Official price index Price-to-income ratio Price-to-rent ratio
  1. 1992 Q1 · End of the 1985–1991 boom (end of a boom)
    The index starts inside the fall that followed the 1985–1991 boom in Île-de-France, which the sources describe as a speculative bubble. Real prices in Paris fell by nearly 46% between 1991 and 1997.
    Sources: Revue Banque, 2005; Grjebine, CEPII, L'économie mondiale 2014.
  2. 2008 Q3 · Global financial crisis (financial or economic crisis)
    First peak of the cycle in 2008 Q3, followed by a short fall during the global financial crisis; prices were rising again by the end of 2009.
    Sources: Notaires de Paris-Île-de-France, press release 23 Feb 2012.
  3. 2011 Q3 · Euro-area debt crisis, tighter credit, capital-gains tax reform (tax or lending rule)
    Both ratios peak, then prices move sideways until 2012 Q3 and drift down. Lenders tightened conditions during the sovereign debt crisis, and the capital-gains tax reform of 1 February 2012 pulled sales forward into January 2012, after which volumes dropped.
    Sources: FNAIM, press release 3 Jan 2012; Notaires de Paris-Île-de-France, market at end February 2012.
  4. 2020 Q3 · Peak, then the rise in interest rates from 2022 (interest rates)
    Highest level of the index and of both ratios. The notaries attribute the fall in sales and prices to the rise in mortgage rates since early 2022 and the loss of borrowing capacity. The first part of the fall, in 2020–2021, has no verified cause in the sources read.
    Sources: Notaires du Grand Paris, press conference 7 Sept 2023, as reported (secondary source).

Numbers mark turning points of the series; the text gives what the sources say happened around each date. These are coincidences in time documented by the sources listed, not causal estimates of this project, and several explanations can hold at once. Ratios are divided by their own average over the period shown, so 100 means "usual for this market", not "fair value".

Market tension and liquidity

Rents are a constrained expense: households must be housed, so a rent-based value can "justify" high prices while the market is stuck. When prices are too high, sellers rarely cut them at once; sales volumes fall first. This section tracks that adjustment. Time on market would be the ideal measure, but it needs listing data, which are not open.
