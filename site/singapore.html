Singapore (HDB)

Resale transactions of public (HDB) flats on 99-year leases, January 2017 onward. Rents are HDB approvals to rent out whole flats. Fundamental value discounts rent over the remaining lease, not in perpetuity.

The valuation gap compares the model's predicted market price with rents capitalised at an assumed user cost. A positive value means prices are above what rents justify under the baseline assumptions; see the sensitivity band below. It is a model-dependent hypothesis, not investment advice.

Relative mispricing is the median residual of the price model: positive = dearer than comparable dwellings elsewhere in the city, negative = cheaper. It says nothing about a bubble.

HDB rents are available from 2021; earlier years use the rent model's 2021 level and should be read with care. A negative gap does not mean HDB flats are a bargain anyone can take: only eligible households (mainly citizens) may buy, owners must live in the flat for 5 years before renting it out, and grants and CPF rules shape demand, so rent capitalisation is constrained. Private condominiums (URA) are not included yet.
