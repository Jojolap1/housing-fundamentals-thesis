Sales of private apartments and condominiums (the open market: no eligibility rules, buy-to-let allowed), November 2021 onward, and the rental contracts stamped with IRAS. This is the Singapore market most comparable with Paris.

The valuation gap compares the model's predicted market price with rents capitalised at an assumed user cost. A positive value means prices are above what rents justify under the baseline assumptions; see the sensitivity band below. It is a model-dependent hypothesis, not investment advice.

Relative mispricing is the median residual of the price model: positive = dearer than comparable dwellings elsewhere in the city, negative = cheaper. It says nothing about a bubble.

Source: URA private residential transactions and rental contracts (URA Data Service), Census of Population 2020. Period shown: the selected year. Cells with fewer than 5 transactions in a year are not published. Valuation gaps are model-dependent hypotheses, not investment advice.
