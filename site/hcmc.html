Ho Chi Minh City (exploratory)

Administrative land values, not market prices. These are the land prices set by the city for taxes, fees and compensation (Resolution 87/2025/NQ-HĐND of the HCMC People's Council, in force since 1 January 2026). They are neither transaction prices nor asking prices, they cover land only, and they have historically been well below market values. Vietnam publishes no transaction register and no rents, so the rent-capitalisation test run for Paris and Singapore cannot be run here.
